# Marketing follow-ups

Two parked initiatives from the launch session. Each section is self-contained so future-you doesn't have to reconstruct context.

---

## 📹 YouTube channel — app walkthrough videos

**When:** within the next 2-4 weeks. Earlier is better — the site is live, Search Console is collecting impressions, and a tutorial video is the single highest-leverage piece of content for a niche tool.

**Why this matters:**
- Long-tail SEO: queries like "how to organise vj clips" and "resolume library manager tutorial" have low competition and high intent.
- Embedded YouTube videos give the marketing site real dwell time, which Google reads as a quality signal.
- VJs are visual learners. A 5-minute video sells the workflow better than 5 paragraphs of copy.

### Channel setup (one-time, ~30 min)

- [ ] Create the channel: **Stagehand VJ** (or use a personal channel if you already have one)
- [ ] Banner: reuse `assets/hero-platforms.webp` cropped to 2560×1440
- [ ] Channel handle: `@stagehandvj` if available
- [ ] Channel description: lead with "A clip library manager for Resolume-based VJ workflows" + link to stagehandvj.com
- [ ] Add "Stagehand VJ" + "stagehandvj.com" to the channel's "Links" section

### First three videos (the ones to make first)

1. **"Stagehand VJ in 2 minutes"** — 90-120s overview. Hook (clip chaos) → drop folder → tag → search → load to Resolume. No talking head, just screen capture + caption overlays. Embed this on the site's `/tutorial` section.

2. **"Setting up your clip library from scratch"** — 5-8 min walkthrough. Real folder of clips, real tagging, real ratings, real collections. Mention: auto-tag-from-folder-names, batch tagging, the "tick what to apply" panel.

3. **"Loading clips into Resolume during a live set"** — 4-6 min. Pin-on-top, layer/column targeting, Ctrl+Enter shortcut, Ctrl+Shift+V to pull clips back. Connect to a real Resolume composition.

### Production checklist per video

- [ ] Script in advance (even bullet points). Don't wing the screen captures.
- [ ] Record at 1920×1080 minimum, 60 fps if your clips are 60 fps
- [ ] Audio: even a $50 mic is a huge step up from laptop mic. Cut filler words ruthlessly.
- [ ] Cold-open in the first 5 seconds — show the payoff (clip loading into Resolume) before any explanation
- [ ] End-screen: link to stagehandvj.com + the next video
- [ ] Description: include `https://stagehandvj.com` on line 1, plus chapter timestamps
- [ ] Tags: `vj`, `resolume`, `vj software`, `clip library`, `dxv`, `visual performance`
- [ ] Pinned comment with the download link and licence info

### Where to share each new video

- The marketing site (embed under a new `/tutorials` anchor section)
- r/Resolume, r/VJing
- Resolume official forum (forum.resolume.com)
- Resolume Discord
- LinkedIn if you have a relevant network
- Twitter/X + Bluesky with screenshots + `#vj` `#resolume`

## 📦 Press package for influential VJs

**When:** 2-3 months after the YouTube videos exist. You need:
1. The app at 1.0 (or genuinely close — no obvious bugs in the demo)
2. At least one polished demo video they can embed/share
3. A few real testimonials from beta users (even informal ones)
4. Cloudflare Analytics showing the site is at least functional

Sending press outreach before these exist is a one-shot you can't redo with the same person.

### What the press package should contain

A single hosted page (could be `/press` on the site) plus a downloadable zip with:

- [ ] **One-paragraph pitch** (50 words max) — what Stagehand is, who it's for, why now
- [ ] **One-page PDF brief** — the pitch, key features, pricing, links, contact email
- [ ] **3-5 high-res screenshots** — the existing `tools/sources/*.png` work; also export a few from the moments section
- [ ] **2-3 short GIF/video clips** showing key workflows (already have these, just re-export at higher quality)
- [ ] **Logo pack** — `logo-mark.png` at multiple sizes, plus a wordmark PNG if you make one
- [ ] **Founder bio** — 2-3 sentences. "I'm a VJ. I built this because…" Personal and short.
- [ ] **Pre-written tweet/post copy** — make it zero-effort to share
- [ ] **A complimentary licence key** they can activate immediately

### Who to send it to (research before pitching)

Categories, not specific names — those change. Spend 30 min identifying current active people in each:

- [ ] **Pro VJs with educational followings** — YouTube channels who do Resolume tutorials, VJ Discord/Twitter personalities with engaged audiences
- [ ] **VJ collectives + residency artists** — people who do regular shows and would actually use it
- [ ] **VJ podcasts / interview series** — there are 3-4 active ones at any time, search "vj podcast 2026"
- [ ] **Festival visual directors** — the people who book VJs at Boom, MUTEK, Sónar, Decibel etc. Long-shot but high payoff
- [ ] **Resolume themselves** — they sometimes feature compatible tools in their newsletter. Email `community@resolume.com` with the press kit
- [ ] **MadMapper / TouchDesigner / Smode communities** — adjacent tools, overlapping audiences

### Pitch email template (rough)

Subject: `Stagehand VJ — a clip library manager for Resolume (free demo)`

Body: 4-6 sentences max. What it is, why you built it, what makes it interesting to *their* audience specifically (do this research — generic blasts get ignored), free licence attached, link to press page.

### Things to NOT do

- ❌ Mass-blast a generic email. Personalised one-off emails get ~10× the response rate.
- ❌ Ask for a review. Ask if they'd be interested in trying it — the review (if any) comes naturally.
- ❌ Send before the app is genuinely solid. A buggy first impression with an influencer kills the lead permanently.
- ❌ Follow up more than once. If they don't respond after two emails, drop it and move on.
